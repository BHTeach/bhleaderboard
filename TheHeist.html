Operation: Midnight

Secret Mission Rules

The Target

Your team broke into another company's building to steal a secret item. But an alarm is ringing! The building will lock down and the police will arrive in 65 minutes. Crack the network, open the vault, and find an escape route before time runs out, or you go to jail.

Communication

  • Talk to other teams to find the passwords.
  • Workplace tools (Zoom, Slack etc.) are OK.
  • You can use smartphones or laptops.
  • You can use many devices at the same time.

Rules

  • Outside tools are allowed (e.g. Google).
  • You MUST NOT share photos or video of the papers.
  • Penalty: If you speak Japanese, your team cannot speak for 2 minutes.
