The Situation

A huge storm has hit the Manor House, and the power is out. We have 65 minutes before the food spoils. You must start the generator before the time runs out. If you fail, dinner is cancelled!

Communication

  • Talk to other departments to find clues.
  • Workplace tools (Zoom, Slack etc.) are ok.
  • You can use smartphones or laptops.
  • You can use many devices at the same time.

Rules

  • Outside resources are allowed (e.g. Google).
  • You may not share photos/video of the documents. These are confidential.
  • Penalty: Using Japanese mutes your department for 2 minutes.
